Safety & supervision

Peace of mind for your team

  1. Supervised at all times

    Every session is run by trained coaches and staff who stay with the group from check-in to pick-up.

  2. Screened, trained coaches

    Our coaches go through rigorous screening, background checks and specialized coach training before they lead a group.

  3. Clear rules & routines

    Every session has the same expectations and structure, so kids know exactly what to expect and can focus on playing.

  4. Built for their age

    Activities are developmentally appropriate and structured to reduce risk, with kids grouped so everyone is matched well.

  5. Everyone belongs

    Coaches actively watch group dynamics and participation, and our programs are designed around positive behavior and inclusion.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What ages and grades do you work with?

Our programs serve elementary and middle school students. Groups are organized so kids are matched with others their age and ability.

Where do programs take place?

Most programs run right on your school’s campus, on its field or basketball court, so there’s no extra drop-off. Camps run during school breaks.

Which sports do you offer?

Basketball, soccer, volleyball, tennis, flag football, cheerleading and run club, plus multi-sport programs that rotate through games and PE-style activities.

How do you keep students safe?

Programs are supervised at all times by trained coaches and staff. Every coach goes through screening, background checks and specialized training, and each session follows clear rules and routines.

Can the program be customized for our school?

Yes. We build every program around your school’s schedule, space and needs: after school, PE, recess, before school or camps.

How do families register?

Families register online through our Programs page. Some schools manage registration themselves, and we can work either way.

Do you offer financial aid?

Yes. Through the Stanford Park Sports Foundation, our 501(c)(3) nonprofit, we offer scholarships and free programs so every child can take part regardless of financial circumstances.
