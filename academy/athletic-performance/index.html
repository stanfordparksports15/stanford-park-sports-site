Built for every sport

The foundation
for what’s next.

SPS Performance Club is designed to help young athletes become faster, stronger, more explosive, and more confident. Our training focuses on developing fundamental athletic abilities that translate into improved performance across all sports, including basketball, soccer, football, volleyball, and more.

Led by experienced coaches, our program combines speed development, running mechanics, agility drills, coordination, endurance, and age-appropriate strength and conditioning in a positive and motivating environment.

Whether your child is looking to improve their athletic performance or simply develop a stronger foundation for sports, SPS Performance Club provides professional training to help every athlete reach their potential.

After-school, PE, recess, before-school and camp sports programs for Bay Area schools since 2021.
