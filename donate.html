More than a game. A team.

Stanford Park Sports and the Stanford Park Sports Foundation are one team. The same coaches and programs you see at 50+ Bay Area schools are run by a nonprofit whose mission is to help every child stay active, build confidence and love sports.

  • Registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization
  • Scholarships and free programs for families who need them
  • Expanding to more schools serving under-resourced kids
