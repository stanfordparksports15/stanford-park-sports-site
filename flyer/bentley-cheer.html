Cheer

Grades K through 4th or 5thChants, jumps and big smiles!
For the Bentley School team

This winter we are bringing an after school cheer program to Bentley students from kindergarten through 4th or 5th grade. Our coaches lead fun, high energy sessions where students learn cheers, motions and routines while building confidence, teamwork and school spirit.

What students will learn

Cheers & chants

Loud, proud and in sync, with clear voices and sharp motions.

Jumps

Fun jumps taught step by step with good technique.

Routines

Learning and practicing a cheer routine together as a team.

Confidence & teamwork

Performing with energy and supporting every teammate.

Rainy day plan

Cheer is a low maintenance sport that works great in a classroom with just a little open space. On wet days the class moves indoors, with dance and choreography as the main focus:

  • Dance and choreography
  • Sharp arm motions
  • Jumps in place
  • Chants and cheers
Stanford Park Sports runs fun, safe, skill focused programs at 50+ Bay Area schools. Students build skills, confidence and teamwork every week.
