Volleyball

Grades 3 to 5Bumps, sets, serves and a whole lot of fun!
For the Bentley School team

Bentley families asked for volleyball, so this winter we are bringing an after school volleyball program to students in grades 3 to 5. Our coaches teach real volleyball skills in a fun, encouraging way, and students build confidence and teamwork every week.

What students will learn

Passing & setting

Bumps and sets with good form, so the ball goes where they want it to.

Serving

Underhand serves first, then building up as skills and confidence grow.

Footwork

Ready position, quick feet and moving to the ball.

Teamwork

Calling the ball, cheering on teammates and playing real games.

Rainy day plan

Volleyball is a low maintenance sport that works great in a classroom with just a little open space. On wet days the class moves indoors, and no net is needed:

  • Passing with soft volleyballs
  • Volleyball trivia
  • Fun team games that fit the room
